## Earlier findings still relevant
- **One-agent design (earlier round):** home Today / What I did / Suggest + big mic; 3 tabs; adaptive home by user's data (entrepreneur sees КП, mother sees school/doctor, pensioner sees medicines/bills) without role questionnaire; smart groups appear only at ≥5 items and when useful, hide after 30 days unused; "Что я для вас настроил" screen with [Ок]/[Изменить]/[Отменить]; autonomy switch "Спрашивать всегда / Делать мелочи сам" (default "Спрашивать" for new 45+ users); source always one tap away. CEO rules: internal actions free, external/irreversible (send, delete, spend, share with third parties, change existing reminder) only after "Да"; agent restates critical details (time, sum, recipient) before acting; learns corrections as habits; abilities discovered by contextual suggestions; metrics: ≥3 abilities used in 14 days by 40% of actives, suggestion acceptance ≥25%, undo/fix rate <5%, D30 retention for 45+.
- **Asking at start (CEO):** max 3 questions in first conversation: how to address you / language; do you use it for work (company name, requisites, logo — photo of a business card or old КП); allow alarm-like reminders. Telegram and email forwarding offered later when there's a reason.

Reuse: db/queue.py, logs.py redaction, config, consent gate, Protocols Extractor/Transcriber/Embedder, qa/retrieval.py (FTS+vector+RRF) and compose.py, scheduler rules, "untrusted data" and structured-output rules.
